Privacy Policy

Bakery Tycoon · Last updated 18 November 2026

Bakery Tycoon has no accounts and keeps your progress on your own device. We do not run any servers and do not collect personal information ourselves. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Both are run by Google, which collects some information as described below.

Who we are

Bakery Tycoon (the “game”) is published by Slicksage (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the game handles information on Android devices.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store on any server your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The game has no sign-in, no analytics and no server of its own.

The game connects to the internet only to load ads and to make purchases through Google Play. It asks for no permissions that you have to approve. It uses internet access and, through Google's ads software, your device's advertising ID.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress (such as cash, day, upgrades, settings and a record of purchases already delivered) is saved in the game's private storage on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include this saved progress in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad in return for an in-game reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location (such as country or city);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and crash information about the ads software.

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which still use limited information for fraud prevention and measurement.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases of in-game items. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details. When you buy something, Google Play tells the game which item was bought and gives an order number, which the game keeps on your device so the item is delivered once. Google processes your purchase under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Rating the game

The game may occasionally invite you to rate it. The rating window is shown and handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive any information from it beyond the public rating or review you choose to post.

Third parties

The only third party that receives information through the game is Google, for the ads, purchases and ratings described above. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else. The game is distributed through Google Play, which also collects its own information about downloads under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your information

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Your game progress can be deleted by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is managed by Google; you can review and delete it through your Google Account and reset your advertising ID as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has made a purchase without permission, refunds can be requested through Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
