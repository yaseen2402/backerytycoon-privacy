Privacy Policy

Bakery Tycoon · Last updated 13 November 2026

Bakery Tycoon does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The game works fully offline and has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Who we are

Bakery Tycoon (the “game”) is published by Slicksage (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the game handles information on Android devices.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect or transmit:

  • your name, email address or any other contact details;
  • your location, contacts, photos or files;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • usage statistics or crash reports.

The game requests no device permissions and does not connect to the internet.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress (such as cash, day, upgrades and settings) is saved in the game's private storage on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include this saved progress in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

Rating the game

The game may occasionally invite you to rate it. The rating window is shown and handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive any information from it beyond the public rating or review you choose to post.

Third parties

The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no information with third parties. The game is distributed through Google Play, which collects its own information about downloads and purchases under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
